Your feedback table had 21 rows. All 21 are addressed in the build. Fifteen were unambiguous and are simply done. 02

The one we have pushed back on

The Little Mermaid is not ours to publish, and we do not think it should be yours either.

Edvard Eriksen's sculpture is still in copyright, and Denmark has no freedom of panorama for artworks. That means a photograph of the statue counts as a derivative work of the sculpture in Denmark and across the EU. The Eriksen estate is known for enforcing it.

What we found looking for one. The only openly licensed Little Mermaid photograph on Wikimedia Commons has the statue painted out in white, and is filed under the title "compliant with Danish law". Its own description reads: "no Freedom of panorama (FOP) in Denmark. This The Little Mermaid image is in the public domain in the United States." The United States only. Not Denmark, not Australia.

Every other result was either a souvenir van parked in front of the statue or a crowd of tourists photographing it, which is the opposite of the "less busy" you asked for.

So we delivered the brief rather than the instruction. The Team hero is now Amalienborg Palace with the dome of Frederik's Church behind it. Copenhagen, an almost empty plaza, symmetrical, deep blue sky, and a Danish flag flying on the right. It is a good deal emptier and stronger than the Nyhavn shot it replaces, which is what row 9 was asking for.

If you want the mermaid specifically, the route is a commercially licensed shot that clears the sculpture rights, or a photograph you commission and license directly. Both are worth doing properly rather than absorbing the risk. Tell us which and we will make it happen.

03

The one that is not broken

Row 16, the Experience filtering. We tested it before touching it. It works.

Driving it automatically: selecting "Commercial & Transaction" returns 5 projects, "Technology & Change" returns 4, and the project titles genuinely change between filters. No errors anywhere.

It looked broken for two specific reasons, and both are now fixed:

  • The number beside a service said 13, but the grid only ever shows 8 at a time because it paginates. Removing those numbers, which row 15 asked for anyway, removes the mismatch.
  • "All" and "Program & Project" open with the same first three projects, and the grid sits below the fold, so clicking a filter appeared to do nothing. The page now scrolls the results into view whenever you change a filter, so you see the change land.

The "24 projects" count above the grid has stayed, because it is the one honest signal that the filter fired.
