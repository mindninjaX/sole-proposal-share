The whole path, so nothing is a surprise

Recovery is a sequence. Your current step is marked below. If it does not work, use the button on your recommendation to move to the next one, without starting over.

  1. 1
    Reminder
    A short, friendly prompt. Most late payments are just an oversight and stop here.
  2. 2
    Firm follow-up
    A direct message naming a specific payment date and a clear payment link.
  3. 3
    Phone call, then confirm in writing
    When messages stop working, a call gets a commitment. Confirm it the same day.
  4. 4
    Formal letter of demand
    A formal written notice that payment is required, and that recovery may follow.
  5. 5
    Decide: recover, arrange a plan, or write it off
    Weigh the amount owed against the time and cost of chasing it further.

Fewer late payments next time

Most overdue invoices trace back to a slow or unclear start. Five things that fix that.

  1. 01
    Invoice the moment the work is done
    The longer you wait to send it, the longer you wait to be paid.
  2. 02
    Put your payment terms on every invoice
    State the due date and how to pay, in writing, so there is nothing to argue about later.
  3. 03
    Make paying a single tap
    Add a payment link so there is no friction and no excuse to delay.
  4. 04
    Turn on automatic reminders
    Let the system do the chasing before an invoice goes cold.
  5. 05
    Take a deposit on larger jobs
    Get part of the money up front so you are never fully exposed.

Fix the invoicing, not just this invoice

Late payments usually aren't about customers refusing to pay. They happen when invoices go out late, terms aren't clear, or paying is a hassle.

Sole prevents that: fast invoices, clear terms, easy payment, and automatic follow-up before anything is overdue.
