Stage 3 · Run your business

Pricing and getting paid

Now you're set up, here's how to price your work, invoice, and get paid on time. These are habits and templates to use as you go, so there's nothing to tick off.

Set your prices and quotes

Price for profit, not just to win the job. Put your rates in writing and send a clear quote before you start.

Pricing template — coming soon

Send professional invoices

Invoice as soon as the work is done or the order ships. Include your ABN, the amount, GST if you're registered, and how to pay.

Invoice template — coming soon

Agree payment terms and deposits upfront

Set the expectation in writing when you agree the work, and take a deposit on larger jobs to protect yourself if a client pulls out.

Thanks [name], that's all confirmed. Payment is due on completion by card, tap-and-go or bank transfer. Let me know if you have any questions.

Handle changes to a job

If the work or order changes, confirm the new cost in writing before you go ahead.

This change will add [amount] to the original quote. Reply YES to approve and I'll go ahead.

Get paid and chase overdue invoices

Confirm the invoice arrived, follow up the day after it's late, and have a firm final notice ready before any escalation.

Hi [name], just checking on invoice #123 — when can we expect payment? Hi [name], invoice #123 is now 7 days overdue. Please arrange payment by [date] to avoid it being referred for collection. Happy to sort out any issue.
