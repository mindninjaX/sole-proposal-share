Two rules that keep it safe

Transcripts never train the bot directly

Raw calls contain guesses, one customer's edge case, and their private data. Only approved cards become knowledge. That's about 5 cards a day, not 500.

Every card has an owner and a date

Ashish already flagged the real bug: when the screen changes, the doc doesn't, and the bot confidently teaches the old version. A card that can go stale quietly is the thing we're replacing.

Start with chat, not calls. Every chat ever had is already sitting in the support panel — no vendor, no consent forms, no calendars. We can mine cards from it this week. Calls join the loop once recording is sorted (next tab).

Record the call. Don't build Zoom.

Jo wants every session recorded, transcribed and fed back to the AI. Agreed — and we can have it without writing a single line of video code.

Why not build our own video tool

Video is a commodity and a tar pit — media servers, echo cancellation, mobile, bad wifi. It would eat the whole runway on the one part that makes us zero difference. And customers won't install a new call app from their accounting software. They already accept Google Meet.

Three ways to get the transcript

All three end in the same place. Only the middle one changes.

A · Google Meet does itfree-ish

Meet records, Gemini transcribes, files land in Drive.

But: depends on our Workspace tier, someone has to remember to press record, and accents are where it's weakest — the one review on our booking page already mentions accent.

B · Send a note-takerrecommended

One API sends a named bot into the Meet they already booked. It returns audio, transcript and who-said-what to us, by webhook.

Why this one: the transcript lands in our thread automatically instead of in someone's Drive. Nothing to build, priced per hour recorded.

C · Everyone runs an appquickest

Each caller runs a note-taker on their laptop. Great summaries, today.

But: per seat, and the transcripts live in a vendor's silo, so the loop stays manual.

Recording rules differ by state and our customers are everywhere, so: the booking page says it may be recorded · the note-taker announces itself · we store yes / no / not asked on the call · “no” means no recording and Merlin takes notes by hand · audio is deleted on a timer, and names and numbers get stripped when a card is approved. Five small fixes, worth doing this week

Found by using both surfaces as a customer and as an agent. All cheap.

1 · The reply lag

Agent replies take a while to reach the customer, which is long enough to lose them. Show “Merlin is typing…” the instant Take over is pressed, and check whether the widget is polling.

2 · “Online” at 2:33am

Teach the widget our hours.

3 · The human has no name

Customers see a purple bubble labelled A. Make it “Merlin from Sole joined the chat,” with a face.

4 · Bookings vanish

A booked call should appear in the conversation. Right now the panel has no idea it happened.

5 · Plan says “—”

Status says active, plan says nothing. Small data bug, but it's the first thing an agent reads.

+ One calendar

TidyCal for the app, a round-robin for firms. Two calendars, one question: “was this person called?” Pick one, or make them both write to the thread.
