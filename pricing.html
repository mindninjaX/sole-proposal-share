Your clients' side of the ledger

Sole Small Business, and how the referral works

One ledger, two apps

Sole Small Business is the app your clients use: invoicing, quotes, expenses, transactions and GST-ready records, built for sole traders and micro businesses. Your practice runs SPM. Both sit on the same ledger.

  • Their books arrive in your practice already coded, not as a shoebox in July.
  • You see their position between jobs, so advisory conversations start earlier.
  • Client access is included on every SPM plan, at no extra cost to you.

How the referral works

Three steps, and one number that decides the price.

Step 1

Set the client up

Do it for them from the client record you already hold, or send them an invite. Either way it starts inside SPM.

Step 2

They go active

A referral counts once that account is live and being used.

Step 3

Reach 20, pay nothing

Your Start plan is $0 a month for as long as 20 stay active.

You do not have to wait on the client. SPM creates their Sole Small Business account from the record you already hold, so there is no form for them to fill in and no chasing on your side.

The rule, in plain terms

  • 20

    20 is the number, and it is all or nothing. At 20 active referrals the plan is $0. At 19 it is $149 a month. There is no part-way discount, so you always know which side of the line you are on.

  • 19

    Drop below 20 and the plan goes back to $149 until you are back at 20. Nothing is switched off and nothing is lost, only the price changes.

  • Already paid for the year? You are not out of pocket. The referral value comes back as add-on credits you can spend on storage, ID checks or extra client blocks.

  • Your count sits on your billing page. You can see how many are active and how many you are short, any day of the month.
